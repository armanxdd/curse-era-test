A storyboard sheet: one square image with six cells in a grid of three columns and two rows, read left to right, top row first. Thin white gutters between the cells. 6 panels are used. Every panel is a vertical frame with the camera upright, the floor at the bottom and the characters upright; not rotated, not sideways, not a top-down view. No text, no numbers and no captions anywhere, except the words a panel below allows.

STORY of the whole ad: Mama Zuri, an orc mom over 40 in a jungle village, has a big belly and FUPA. By the waterfall she takes her first Bitte shot and golden magic starts melting the belly away on screen. Over the week it keeps shrinking while she still eats ice cream and chocolate, whatever the village elders say. One Bitte shot a day flushes the junk out of her gut; from then on she is lean, flat-stomached and full of energy, and loves the tropical taste, unlike the bitter SOURLEAF drops a vendor tries. The whole village ends up holding Bitte, and she grabs one of the last bottles and points down to the link. This sheet shows part 6 of 7 (the village's energy, the sunset toast and the junk food over the waterfall).

Image 4 is the character sheet of Kofi, market vendor. Image 5 is the character sheet of Imani, the lead's friend. Image 6 is the Bitte bottle photo. Image 7 is the Bitte shot photo, for the colour of the liquid only: never its glass bottle, its room or its person. Image 8 is a style reference frame: for the rendering and the orc character design only, never its faces, product or text.

STYLE: Glossy semi-realistic 3D animated feature-film style. Green-skinned orc characters with smooth, detailed skin, small pointed elf-like ears, small lower tusks that show when they smile, big expressive friendly faces, slightly ugly but cute, never scary. A lush fantasy jungle with turquoise waterfalls, mossy stone ruins, wooden plank walkways, hanging red heliconia flowers and a thatched jungle village market. Warm golden-hour sun beams through haze, soft cinematic rim light, shallow depth of field, rich warm colour. The Bitte bottle and the shot are not stylised: they stay exactly as in their reference photos.

CHARACTERS (keep each character exactly as in its character sheet): Mama Zuri, the lead: a woman of about 45 with Black features, drawn as an orc: warm sage-green skin with a deep olive-brown undertone, a round friendly face with full cheeks, a broad nose, full lips, warm dark-brown eyes with soft laugh lines, small pointed ears with small gold hoop earrings, two small lower tusks that show when she smiles. Dark-brown natural coily hair in a high puff bun with loose curls at the temples, wrapped at the base with a terracotta fabric band. Outfit: a terracotta-orange cotton top with thin straps and a normal length (never a crop top), a brown fabric wrap skirt over brown shorts, a thin bead necklace, simple leather sandals. Her LEAN body: slim and softly toned, feminine, never muscular, bulky or veiny; a flat stomach, the same terracotta top fitting snugly against it, the wrap skirt flat on her hips. Auntie Nala, village elder: a woman of about 60 with Black features, drawn as an orc: warm sage-green skin with a deep brown undertone, a long oval face, high cheekbones, reading glasses on a cord, small pointed ears, short grey coily hair under a green-and-gold patterned headwrap, a loose mustard-yellow kaftan dress, wooden bead bracelets. Uncle Baako, village elder: a man of about 62 with Black features, drawn as an orc: olive-green skin with a warm brown undertone, a broad square face, a short white beard, bushy white eyebrows, small pointed ears, two small lower tusks, bald on top with short white hair at the sides, a cream linen shirt and an earthy brown wrap cloth over trousers, a carved wooden walking stick. Kofi, market vendor: a man of about 45, mixed ethnicity, drawn as an orc: lighter moss-green skin with a golden undertone, an oval face, a short neat black beard, short black twists, small pointed ears, two small lower tusks, a sleeveless olive-green tunic over brown trousers, a leather cord necklace, an everyday build, not muscular. Imani, the lead's friend: a woman of about 42 with Black features, drawn as an orc: deep emerald-green skin with a dark-brown undertone, a heart-shaped face, a wide bright smile, small pointed ears, two small lower tusks, shoulder-length box braids with gold cuffs, a sleeveless coral-pink wrap dress, wooden bangles; slim and softly curvy, never muscular.

THE BITTE BOTTLE (its reference photo): a white, opaque bottle with a soft matte finish, a rounded rectangular body with softly bevelled corners, a short neck and a wide white screw cap. Its white label shows "BITTE" in small orange capitals at the top, "SOURSOP BITTER SHOTS" in large dark green capitals, a small taste chart, an orange band reading "IMMUNITY + ENERGY", and botanical drawings of soursop fruit and leaves at the bottom. SIZE: small, about as tall as an adult hand from wrist to fingertips (about 18 cm), clearly shorter than a forearm; never oversized. Never a clear or glass bottle. Do not redraw, restyle or re-letter the label.

THE BITTE SHOT: bright yellow-orange, slightly thick, opaque liquid, poured from the white Bitte bottle into a small clear shot glass, filled about three quarters. One shot glass unless the line says otherwise.

Nobody talks, sings or mouths words: every reaction is a silent facial expression. No brands or logos on anything except the Bitte bottle.

PANELS:
Panel 1 (top left, sung: "More energy,"): Medium shot in the market square: Auntie Nala and Uncle Baako dance happily, each with a white Bitte bottle in hand, while Kofi strides past behind them with a heavy fruit basket on his shoulder, easily.
Panel 2 (top middle, sung: "stronger immunity, less fatigue."): Medium shot in a sunny thatched hut: Imani wakes up fresh and stretches with a bright smile, a white Bitte bottle on the bedside table.
Panel 3 (top right, sung: "All you need is one thing,"): Medium wide shot at sunset on a cliff above the waterfall: Mama Zuri in her LEAN body, Imani and Kofi raise small clear shot glasses of the bright yellow-orange Bitte shot, joyful; the white Bitte bottle on the rock.
Panel 4 (bottom left, sung: "one shot a day:"): Close-up: three small clear shot glasses of the bright yellow-orange Bitte shot clink together against the sunset, the white Bitte bottle on the rock below, label to the camera.
Panel 5 (bottom middle, sung: "Soursop Bitters from Bitte"): Medium shot at the top of the waterfall: Mama Zuri in her LEAN body lifts a woven basket of junk food (burgers, pizza, sweets, no brands) with a triumphant grin, a white Bitte bottle in her other hand.
Panel 6 (bottom right, sung: "flush that crap diet away!"): Wide shot: the junk food tumbles over the waterfall while Mama Zuri laughs, holding the white Bitte bottle up.
